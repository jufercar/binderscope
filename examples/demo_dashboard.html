Pipeline

Multi-criteria ranking

Each metric is min-max normalised to [0–1] in its own good direction and multiplied by its weight; the score is the sum. Maximum possible score: 15.5. Weights encode priorities and live in the config file.

Rows are kept for ranking only when dG < 0, so artefacts do not stretch the normalisation scale.
